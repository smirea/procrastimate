# Add a task

Add a task lets Stefan capture a task in one motion from anywhere in the app. Recognized phrases for due date, priority, reminders, project, and labels are highlighted inline as he types, removed from the title, and applied to the task, which lands in the right list at once.

## Sub-features

- `add-open` opens quick add from each entry point.
- `add-save` saves a task with a title and lands it in the current list.
- `add-due` parses a due date typed into the title, such as `tomorrow`, `fri 5pm`, a month name date such as `Dec. 25`, `20th of October`, `Monday, October 19`, `Oct 19, 10am`, or a spelled-out day such as `October twentieth`, `the third of May`, or `the fifteenth`, `the 1st of the month` or `the first of the month` as the next 1st, the nth weekday of a month such as `the 1st Monday`, `the last Friday`, or `the first Monday of November`, a bare hour after `at` such as `November 3rd at 10` (10am), and `@` for `at` as in `Oct 2 @ 3pm`.
- `add-shorthand` parses compact phrases such as `tom 5p`, `eow`, `2d`, `5m`, `1730`, `10/15`, and `the 15th`, and leaves names and ordinary words such as `Tom` and `sun hat` as text. An offset past the year 9999, such as `3929284d`, also stays text.
- `add-recurrence` parses a repeat such as `every mon`, `daily`, yearly `every march 2nd` or `every March second`, or a weekday list such as `mon wed fri 7am` or `tue/thu`, shows it as a chip, and stores it on the task. A monthly repeat on a day, such as `every 3rd`, `every month on the twentieth`, or `every 31st`, reads `Every month on the 3rd` and lands on the last day of a shorter month. A monthly repeat on a weekday, such as `every 3rd Monday` or `every last Friday`, reads `Every month on the 3rd Mon`. `every other Tuesday` repeats every 2 weeks on Tuesday. `every 5th Monday` stays text whole. A repeat longer than 999 units, such as `every 99999999mo`, stays text. A weekday list in prose, such as `Discuss mon wed plan`, stays text, and so do `every 3rd day` and `the third option`.
- `add-highlight` highlights each parsed phrase inline and shows its value as a chip.
- `add-due-picker` sets the date and time from the date chip: preset tiles, a calendar, and a time view that commits only a picked time.
- `add-config-row` spells out the resolved due date, repeat, and reminders in the configuration row below the field while the text carries them.
- `add-defaults` starts quick add with the context of the screen it opens on, from `screenDefaults` in `shared/screens.ts` (`Screens.defaults` on iOS): the project in a project, the section from a section's add row (web) or heading menu (iOS), the label in a label view, the place on a place screen, and the event on an event screen. An Inbox day heading's `+` (web) or `Add task` (iOS heading menu) starts on that day, and a No date place heading's `Add task` on iOS starts at that place. Inbox, No date, and Completed start bare. The web's `q`, floating button, sidebar `Add task`, and in-list add rows, and the iOS tab bar's `+` (pinned tabs included), all read the same map. On the web a place or event shows as a chip with a remove button, since only iOS can pick one; an event hides the date and repeat chips.
- `add-cancel` discards an unsaved draft.
- `add-reveal` closes quick add after save and shows the task's list, scrolled to the new row, which flashes. The list is the open screen when it holds the task (`screenHolds`), else the task's project, else Inbox for a dated task or No date for an undated one.
- `add-create` offers `Create #name` or `Create @name` in the chip row for an unknown project or label and creates it only when picked.
- `add-dock` attaches quick add to the keyboard on a phone and hides the add button while it is open.
- `add-chip-row` keeps the attribute chips on one line that scrolls sideways, with a `+` after it whose menu lists every action, opens the picked one's picker in the row, and pins or unpins buttons for every device.
- `add-subtasks` takes subtask lines under the title from the checklist chip, parses each like `Add subtask`, removes a line with its `×`, and saves them under the new task in order.
- `add-grow` grows the title with long text, to ten lines on iOS and twelve on the web (less when a phone web screen has no room above the keyboard), or four while the subtask list is open, with the subtask field at three, then scrolls with the caret kept in view, including after a paste.
- `add-toast` moves a toast to the top of a phone screen while quick add is open, so it never covers the chips.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Web: choose `Add task` in the sidebar or at the bottom of any task list.
- Web: press `q` while focus is outside a text field.
- Web on a phone: tap the floating `Quick add` button. Quick add opens as a bottom sheet docked above the on-screen keyboard.
- iOS: tap `Quick add`, the accent `+` circle beside the tab bar. It opens quick add without switching tabs. In an XCUITest, tap it as `app.buttons["quick-add-button"]`, its identifier: the quick add card carries the identifier `Quick add`, so matching that string can resolve to the card instead. Lists have no inline `Add task` row and no floating button (see `docs/decisions/ios.md`).

## Driving it with control-ui and the simulator

Preconditions:

- The baseline state, with an empty Inbox. Steps assume the clock reads Wednesday, October 14 2026, 10:00 UTC.

- **Open quick add.** Press `q` on the web Inbox. The `Quick add` dialog appears with focus in the `Task name` field.
- **Highlight and save.** Type `call mom tomorrow 5pm remind me 30m before p1`. `tomorrow 5pm`, `remind me 30m before`, and `p1` are highlighted, the chips read `Tomorrow 5pm` and `30m before`, and the priority flag is filled red. Press Enter. Quick add closes and `call mom` flashes under Inbox's `Tomorrow` with `Tomorrow 5pm` and one reminder.
- **Every field.** Type `Call mom tomorrow 5pm p1 #Errands @phone remind me 30m before`, then choose `Create #Errands` and `Create @phone`. `tomorrow 5pm`, `p1`, `#Errands`, `@phone`, and `remind me 30m before` are highlighted, and the chips read `Due Tomorrow 5pm`, `Priority 1`, `Project Errands`, `Labels phone`, and `30m before`. Press Enter; `Errands` opens with `Call mom` showing `Tomorrow 5pm` and `phone`. After a reload its details read the title `Call mom`, `Project Errands`, `Due Tomorrow 5pm`, `Priority 1`, `Labels phone`, and a `30m before` reminder. Test: `quick add parses a date, priority, reminder, #project, and @label and saves each on the task` in `e2e/quick-add.e2e.ts`.
- **Reveal.** From Inbox, save `Buy milk`. Quick add closes, No date opens, and the `Buy milk` row flashes. Save `Pay rent tomorrow`; Inbox opens with it under `Tomorrow`. From a project, save `Fix sink`; the project stays open with the row flashing. Save `Call mom tomorrow #Errands` from Inbox; Inbox stays open with it under `Tomorrow`, since Inbox lists it. On iOS a project task saved where it doesn't show opens its project in Browse unless that project is already open.
- **Due date.** Add `Call mom tomorrow`. The task title reads `Call mom` and shows `Tomorrow`.

Drive the web in the browser and iOS in the simulator.
- **Subtasks.** Type `Pack for trip tomorrow`, then click the checklist chip `Add subtask`; the `Add subtask` field opens under the title with focus. Type `Passport today p1`, Enter, `Mistake`, Enter, `Charger every day`, Enter. The list reads `Passport` with `Today` and a red ring, `Mistake`, and `Charger` with `Today` and `Every day`, and the chip reads `Add subtask, 3 added`. Click `Remove Mistake`. ## Gotchas

- Pressing `q` inside a text field types the letter instead of opening quick add.
- Browser devtools cannot open a real on-screen keyboard. Simulate one by overriding `visualViewport.height` and firing `resize`, and confirm keyboard docking on a real iPhone.
- Date words parse relative to the device clock and time zone. Assert the date the app shows, not one you computed. Pin the clock rather than reading the wall clock.
- `docs/decisions/quick-add.md` lists every phrase, default, and guard. `bun test shared/quick-add.test.ts` proves each one with the clock pinned. Use those tests for a phrase-by-phrase check instead of the UI.
- A time with no day lands tomorrow once it has passed. At the pinned 10:00, `9am` reads `Tomorrow 9am`.
- A date, recurrence, priority, or project phrase that appears twice uses the last one. `Today task today` saves `Today task` due today.
- The highlight layer sits behind the input and is `aria-hidden`. Find tokens with the `[data-token="due"]`, `recurrence`, `priority`, `reminder`, `project`, and `label` selectors, and read values from the chips.
- A task that appears before a reload proves only local state. Reload or relaunch for persistence.
