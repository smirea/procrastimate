# Places and events

A task can hang off a place or a calendar event, set with the Location and Event chips in task details or quick add on the iPhone, never by typing. A place comes from an Apple Maps search and fires on arrival, on leaving, or shows a Live Activity of the place's tasks while Stefan is there. An event comes from the iPhone's calendar and puts the task on a moment of it: before, at the start, during, or after, with reminders counting back from that moment. Moved events move their tasks. Places and events with open tasks get their own Browse sections above Labels. Neither has quick add or Ramble phrases. Quick add and Ramble on a place or event screen, on the web too, save the new task at that place or on that event.

## Sub-features

- `place-pick` sets a place from the `Location` chip: an Apple Maps search with `Arrive`, `Leave`, or `During` beside the field. The watched radius fits the result: 150 m for a shop or address, about half the city for a city (`Berlin` is about 22 km), capped at 100 km. The current-place map zooms out to show the whole circle.
- `place-radius` adjusts a set place's radius in its Location sheet: `Block`, `Neighborhood`, and `City` presets and a `Radius` slider from 100 m to 100 km, with the circle following live. A `Fitted` preset leads them once the radius differs from the one fitted at pick time, unless that size is already one of the presets, and restores it; it stays for the rest of the sheet after it is tapped. The web shows the radius after the place in the details chip (`While at Tiergarten 1.5 km`).
- `place-unwatched` turns the chip's pin into an amber warning (accessibility label ending `, not watched`) and shows a note under the radius when iOS refuses to watch a place or it is past the 20 armed soonest. On the simulator iOS refuses every place, so the note always shows there.
- `place-permission` asks for location access the first time a place is set, and asks to upgrade to Always.
- `place-arrive` notifies on arriving, `place-leave` on leaving, never for the state the phone was already in when the place was set. A crossing that reaches several tasks shows one notification, `4 tasks at Austin` or `2 tasks leaving Austin`, listing the first three titles, and tapping it opens the place. Unit test: `arrivingWithSeveralTasksShowsOneNotificationThatOpensThePlace` in `NotificationScheduleTests`. With Always granted this works while the app is suspended or terminated, because the app holds a `CLServiceSession` requiring Always from launch.
- `place-during` starts a Live Activity listing the place's open `During` tasks while inside, and ends it on leaving.
- `place-arming` watches at most 20 places, and a place with a due date or reminders only from the start of its due day or its first reminder.
- `event-pick` links a calendar event from the `Event` chip, with the moment and minutes, and `Only this one` for a repeating event.
- `event-replaces-date` hides the date and repeat chips while an event is linked and drops the due date and repeat; the reminder chip offers only presets before the event's moment.
- `event-sync` rewrites a linked event's times when it moves in the calendar.
- `rows` show the event line (`15m after Standup`, tinted by its day) and the place line (`Arriving at Whole Foods Market`).
- `browse-sections` lists Places and Events above Labels while open tasks use them, each opening its tasks. Saved places list with the labels instead.
- `place-save` names a place from `Save place` in its screen's `…` menu (on iOS with the map and radius). It then lists with the labels under a pin, opens its label screen (name, radius, address), and its open tasks take the name and radius. iOS `Edit place` on that screen changes the radius. A name a label has is refused.
- `place-at-name` sets a saved place by typing `@name` in quick add or the task title (a `place` token), or by checking it in the labels picker, on both clients. Unchecking clears the place. The `@` suggestion rows, the labels chip, search, and Ramble draw a pin for a saved place, never a tag. An AI account saves one with `POST /api/labels` and a `place`, resizes it with `PATCH /api/labels` and `radius`, and `@name` in `POST /api/tasks` text sets the task's place (`an AI account saves a place, resizes it, and puts tasks at it with @name` in `server/src/sharing.test.ts`).
- `place-choices` lists `Recent` (3), `Saved` (all), and `Other places` (up to 20) in the iOS Location sheet before anything is typed.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- iOS: open a task, tap `Location` (after the reminder chip), search, pick `Arrive`, `Leave`, or `During`, and tap a result. Tap the chip again to change the trigger or `Remove location`.
- iOS: open a task, tap `Event`, pick the moment and an upcoming event, and tap `Save`. Tap the chip again to change it or `Remove event`.
- iOS: Browse tab, `Places` and `Events` sections.
- Web: open a place in the sidebar's Places section and use `Save` to name it; type `@name` in quick add or check the place in a task's `Labels` picker.
- Web: shows the place and event lines on rows, read-only `During Walking meetup · Oct 21 6pm` and `While at Tiergarten` chips in task details (the event chip replaces the date and repeat pickers), and `Places` and `Events` sidebar sections above Labels opening `/places/<key>` and `/events/<id>`. It cannot set either.
- Web check: seed tasks with `location` and `event` from a second device through `/api/upload` (see Baseline preconditions in the README); they stream in without a reload. Inbox places `Night out` under its day as `<time> (30m before [calendar] Night out)`, and a weekly task at a place due last week shows on its next occurrence instead of overdue. The Inbox placement is manual. The radius label is unit-tested through `formatRadius` in `shared/triggers.test.ts`. Saved places: `a place saved under a name lists with the labels, and @name or the labels picker puts a task there` in `e2e/places.e2e.ts`.

## Driving it with simctl

Preconditions:

- A booted iOS 27 simulator, the app built with `xcodebuild` for it and installed. `UDID` is the simulator's identifier.
- `./sim` (and `app-ios/scripts/run` for any simulator) grants Always location, calendar, and notifications after installing and before launching, so no permission alert covers the screen. By hand, after the app is installed: `xcrun simctl privacy $UDID grant location-always com.stefan.procrastimate`, the same for `calendar`, and `applesimutils --byId $UDID --bundle com.stefan.procrastimate --setPermissions notifications=YES`, since `simctl` cannot grant notifications. A `simctl` grant made before the app is installed does not stick, and an alert already on screen stays until the simulator reboots.
- The DEBUG launch key `PROCRASTIMATE_DEMO` stands in for taps, as space-separated words: `seed` adds `Standup` (daily) and `Dentist` to the calendar and seeds `Buy oat milk` (arrive at Whole Foods Market), two `During` tasks at Philz Coffee, `Post standup notes` (15m after Standup), and `Floss first` (1h before Dentist); `browse` opens Browse; `nodate` opens the No date tab; `details:<title with + for spaces>` opens a task; `sheet:location` or `sheet:event` opens that chip's sheet; `query:<words>` fills the Maps search on the first open of the location sheet only, so a reopen shows the picked place and its radius; `quickadd` opens quick add; `ramble` opens the Ramble drawer listening; `search` opens Browse with its search field focused; `type:<text with + for spaces>` types into the focused field through `UIKeyInput` one character at a time, then logs `PlacesDemo typed: …`; `move` moves the next Standup an hour later and logs the task's occurrences before and after. Pass it as `SIMCTL_CHILD_PROCRASTIMATE_DEMO`, with `SIMCTL_CHILD_PROCRASTIMATE_SIGNED_IN=1`.

- **Seed and Browse.** Launch with `seed browse`. Browse shows `Places` (`Philz Coffee 2`, `Whole Foods Market 1`) and `Events` (`Standup 1`, `Dentist 1`) above Labels. Screenshot with `xcrun simctl io $UDID screenshot`.
- **Rows.** Launch with `seed`. Event rows read like `4:15pm ([repeat] 15m after [calendar] Standup)`: the task's moment in its due tone, then in gray the repeat icon when the task follows a series, the lead, the calendar icon, and the event title. The moment is the edge applied to the occurrence, with day and time joined by a space. It keeps the day even under a day heading. It is worded like any due date but drops the day for today (`4:15pm`, `Tomorrow 2pm`, `Tuesday 8:30am`, `Oct 21 6pm`), and has no time for an all-day event. `Post standup notes` reads `<moment> ([repeat] 15m after [calendar] Standup)` with `1` reminder, and `Floss first` reads `<moment> (1h before [calendar] Dentist)`. Their moments depend on the day the seed ran. In the No date tab, `Buy oat milk` and the two Philz Coffee tasks sit in one list with no heading. Event rows have no postpone swipe.
- **Details chips.** `details:Post+standup+notes` shows the event chip first with no `Date` or `Repeat` chip. `details:Buy+oat+milk` shows `Date`, `Repeat`, `Priority`, `Remind`, `Arriving at Whole Foods Market`, and `Event`.
- **Sheets.** `details:Buy+oat+milk sheet:location` shows the map of the current place with the radius presets and slider at `150 m`, the not-watched note, and `Remove location`. Tapping `City` widens the circle to `10 km` and saves it. To see `Fitted`, start with `details:Buy+oat+milk sheet:location query:Golden+Gate+Park`, pick the park (it fits 900 m), reopen the chip, tap `Clear` (the demo query refills on every open), and tap `Block`. `details:Try+the+seasonal+blend sheet:location query:philz` lists Philz Coffee results with `During` beside the field. `details:Post+standup+notes sheet:event` shows `After` selected, `Minutes after 15m`, `Only this one`, and upcoming events by day with Standup checked.
- **Arrive.** `xcrun simctl location $UDID set 37.3349,-122.0090`, launch, wait 15 seconds, then `xcrun simctl location $UDID set 37.3236,-122.0397`. Within about ten seconds the system banner `Buy oat milk` with body `Arriving at Whole Foods Market` shows, in the open app too (there is no in-app toast), and tapping it opens the task. Several tasks arriving at one place show one banner, like `2 tasks at Whole Foods Market` listing their titles, that opens the place.
- **During.** `xcrun simctl location $UDID set 37.3229,-122.0322` with the app open starts one Live Activity for Philz Coffee; `log show --predicate 'eventMessage CONTAINS "procrastimate.widgets"'` shows the renderer subscribing. Moving away ends it the next time the app runs.
- **Saved places.** Launch with `seed browse` and tap `Philz Coffee` (`browse-place-Philz Coffee`), then `Save place` (`save-place`). Type a name in `place-name` and tap `Save` (`place-save`). The screen becomes the saved place, its rows read `While at <name>`, and Browse lists it under Labels with a pin while Places keeps only Whole Foods Market. Relaunch with `details:Buy+oat+milk sheet:location`: under the current place, `Recent` lists the saved place first with a bookmark (`place-choice-<name>`). Launch with `quickadd type:Grind+beans+@<name>` and the Location chip reads `Arriving at <name>`. Launch with `rows quickadd type:Stretch+@g` and the `@` suggestions list the seeded saved place `gym` under a pin. All of this runs through `./sim test`, since the simulator cannot be tapped otherwise.
- **Moved event.** Launch with `move` and read `PlacesDemo before move` and `after move` from the console: the first occurrence starts an hour later and the series continues.

## Gotchas

- The first state report after a place is set never notifies, so start outside a place before moving into it.
- The iOS 27 simulator has no region monitoring: `CLLocationManager.isMonitoringAvailable(for: CLCircularRegion.self)` is false, and every condition reports `.unmonitored`, so `Arrive`, `Leave`, and `During` crossings, foreground or background, need a device. The steps above describe the device behavior.
- The simulator does not wake a suspended app for a crossing the way a phone does, and `simctl io` screenshots do not show the Dynamic Island or the lock screen. Background delivery and the Live Activity's look need a device.
- A Live Activity can only start while the app is in the foreground. A `During` crossing in the background notifies instead, and the activity starts when the app next opens while still inside.
- Moving one occurrence of a repeating event detaches it, and its identifiers gain `/RID=<date>`. It still belongs to the series.
- The seeded calendar events stay in the simulator's calendar across resets; `seed` adds them only once.
