# Task views

Inbox lists everything dated by day: Overdue, Today, and each later day with dated tasks from every project. Tomorrow lists only tomorrow's tasks, and This week what is overdue through Sunday, by day. No date lists the undated top-level tasks that have no project. Every surface that lists these screens (the web sidebar, iOS Browse, the tab menus, and `Settings > Tabs`) reads one catalog, `listCatalog` in `shared/lists.ts` (see `docs/decisions/lists.md`). Project, label, place, and event lists are covered by their own feature files. Each list animates as tasks arrive and leave.

## Sub-features

- `views-inbox` lists Overdue, Today, and each later day under a heading such as `Tomorrow`. It holds only dated tasks.
- `views-tomorrow` lists tomorrow's tasks in one list with no heading, at `/tomorrow` on the web and as a pin or Browse row on iOS. Quick add there starts tomorrow. Empty, it reads `Nothing tomorrow` with `Tasks due tomorrow show up here.`.
- `views-this-week` lists Overdue, Today, and each later day through Sunday, at `/this-week` on the web and as a pin or Browse row on iOS. Quick add there starts today, or on the heading's day. Empty, it reads `Nothing this week` with `Tasks due by Sunday show up here by day.`.
- `views-catalog` lists the same screens in the same order everywhere: Inbox, Tomorrow, This week, No date, then Projects, Shared with me, Places, Events, and Labels. The web sidebar puts Completed after No date. Empty lists keep their entry.
- `views-no-date` lists undated top-level tasks without a project in one list with no sections, places included. It is an iOS tab and a web sidebar entry at `/no-date` with its count.
- `views-no-date-sort` sorts No date by when each task was added, newest first. Each row shows how long ago at its right: `Today`, `Yesterday`, `3 days ago`, `2 weeks ago`, `5 months ago`, `1 year ago`. The `Newest` button at the top right flips it to `Oldest` and back, and the choice persists per device.
- `views-pinned-tabs` (iOS only) lets `Settings > Tabs` pin, remove, and reorder Inbox, Tomorrow, This week, No date, projects, shared projects and labels, places, events, and labels before Browse, which is always last. Pins persist across relaunches on this device. Tapping the open pin, or long pressing any pin, opens a menu of every screen not already pinned; picking one swaps that tab to it. A pin keeps its tab while its list is empty. When the tabs don't fit beside the `+`, the `+` sits above a full-width bar. Unpinned fixed lists (Inbox, Tomorrow, This week, No date) show as rows at the top of Browse. The web sidebar already lists every screen.
- `views-today` and `views-upcoming` are removed. Their lists are the Inbox's dated sections.
- `views-empty` shows `Nothing scheduled` with `Tasks with a date show up here by day.` on an Inbox with nothing dated. An empty No date shows `Inbox zero` with `Press Q to capture a task.`, or `Tap + to capture a task.` on a phone and iOS.
- `views-counts` shows the Inbox count, overdue plus today, in the web sidebar and as the iOS tab badge. The web sidebar and Browse also count Tomorrow, This week, and No date. The iOS No date tab has no badge.
- `views-pending-count` shows a list's open rows as small faint text in its headers once there are more than 4: right-aligned in each section heading (Inbox and This week days, project sections, a shared link's sections), and beside the page title (web) or in the bar's title (iOS) for Inbox, No date, projects, labels, places, events, and shared links. Subtasks count only where they have a row of their own. Completed tasks are never counted. The number rolls to its new value as tasks complete or arrive.
- `views-scroll-end` scrolls the last row of every list, and the end of Browse and search results, fully clear of the floating add button and the iOS tab bar, with the keyboard up or down. On iOS the room goes away while the keyboard is up.
- `views-compact` (iOS only) shows rows without separators and 6 pt vertical padding. The Inbox root has no navigation bar and fades a small `Inbox` title in once scrolled. Pushed lists use an inline bar with the same small title on scroll.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Web: choose Inbox, Tomorrow, This week, or No date in the sidebar, in that order.
- Web on a phone: tap `Open navigation`, then Inbox. The drawer closes as the view opens. Tapping outside the drawer closes it.
- iOS: tap the Inbox or No date tab. By default the tab bar has Inbox, No date, and Browse, plus the accent `+` circle beside it. Tomorrow and This week are rows at the top of Browse until pinned.

## Driving it with control-ui and the simulator

Preconditions:

- The baseline state. Seed through quick add: with the clock one day earlier, add `Overdue task today`. With the clock back, add `Today task today`, `Later task in 3 days`, and `Someday task`. The steps below assume Wednesday, October 14 2026.
- `e2e/lists.e2e.ts` covers the sidebar order, empty lists staying, and Tomorrow and This week on the web; it saves `test-results/lists/sidebar.png` and `this-week.png`. `listCatalog`, `dueDays`, and the screen rules are unit tests whose vectors `VectorTests` replays on iOS. Drive the rest by hand with `control-ui` on web and through the simulator on iOS.

- **Inbox.** Open Inbox. `Overdue` lists `Overdue task` marked `Yesterday`. `Today` lists `Today task`. `Later task` is under `Saturday`. `Someday task` is not in Inbox. The sidebar count, or the iOS tab badge, reads 2.
- **No date.** Open No date. It lists `Someday task` with `Today` at its right. The web sidebar count reads 1. Choose `Sorted newest first`; it reads `Oldest` and the oldest task leads. Reload; it still reads `Oldest`.
- **Pinned tabs (iOS).** Launch with `PROCRASTIMATE_DEMO="seed rows"` and reset. Browse > Settings > Tabs lists `Inbox`, `No date`, then a muted `Browse`, and offers `Tomorrow` and `This week` first under `Lists`. Tap `Pin Philz Coffee` and `Pin home`; the `+` buttons grey out at 4 pins on a 402 pt iPhone. Close Settings; the bar reads Inbox, No date, Philz Cof…, home, Browse across the full width with the `+` above its trailing end, and Browse scrolls `Completed` clear of the `+`. Relaunch without reset; the pins stay. Tap `Philz Coffee` twice; the menu lists labels `work` and `errands` (not `home`), events, and `Whole Foods Market`. Pick `Whole Foods Market`; that tab now shows it. In Settings > Tabs, delete `No date`; Browse's top gains a `No date` row that pushes the list with a back button. Pin a project with an open task, open its tab, and delete it from `Project actions`; the bar lands on `Inbox` and the project's tab hides, but its pin stays stored. Tap `Undo` on the `Deleted “<name>”` toast; the tab returns with the project's sections and tasks. It also returns when the project comes back through sync (an undo on another device or a restored share). The pin rule: `PinTests` in `app-ios/Tests/CoreTests`. No automated UI spec; drive it with `./sim test`.
- **No sections in No date.** On iOS, launch with `PROCRASTIMATE_DEMO="seed nodate"`. `Buy oat milk`, `Try the seasonal blend`, and `Ask about decaf beans` sit in one list with no `Philz Coffee` heading.
- **Tomorrow and This week.** Add `Call mom tomorrow`, `Pay rent sat`, and `Dentist mon`. Tomorrow lists only `Call mom`; quick add there shows a `Tomorrow` date chip, and `Buy flowers` lands on Tomorrow. This week lists `Today task` under Today, `Call mom` and `Buy flowers` under Tomorrow, `Later task` and `Pay rent` under Saturday, and no `Dentist`, which is next Monday.
- **Other projects.** Add `Project task tomorrow #Work` after creating `Work`. It shows under `Tomorrow` with its project named, and not in No date.
- **Empty lists stay.** Start with no tasks; the sidebar lists Inbox, Tomorrow, This week, No date, and Completed, and the iOS tab bar keeps Inbox and No date. Add `Someday task`, open No date, and complete it; it reads `Inbox zero`, and the entry stays after you open Inbox. An iOS pin to an empty project keeps its tab.
- **Empty.** Complete every task. Inbox shows `Nothing scheduled` with `Tasks with a date show up here by day.`. No date shows `Inbox zero` with `Press Q to capture a task.`, or `Tap + to capture a task.` on a phone.
- **Phone.** Tap `Open navigation`; the `Main` navigation slides in. Tap outside it; it closes. Open Inbox; the same sections show. Open No date; `Someday task` shows.
- **iOS.** The Inbox tab lists the same sections as the web and shows `Nothing scheduled` when empty. The No date tab lists `Someday task` and shows `Inbox zero` when empty. Launch with `PROCRASTIMATE_DEMO=nodate` to open it directly. The Inbox tab carries the overdue plus today count as its badge. Rows have no separators. The bar is hidden at the top, and a small `Inbox` title fades in once the list scrolls. Lists sit on the same tinted canvas as the web with transparent rows.
- **Pending counts.** Create project `Errands` and add 4 tasks; its title has no count. Add a fifth; a faint `5` shows beside `Errands`. Add a section `Later` with 4 tasks; its heading has no count and the title reads `9`. Add a fifth to `Later`; `5` shows at the heading's right and the title reads `10`. Complete one in `Later`; its count goes and the title reads `9`. Tests: `bun run test:e2e -- e2e/pending-count.e2e.ts`, which saves `app-web/test-results/pending-count/project.png`; `swift test` (`VectorTests` views) checks the threshold on iOS. On iOS, `PROCRASTIMATE_DEMO=sections` opens `Home` with `7` in the bar and `5` at the right of `Next up`.
- **Scroll to the end.** With more tasks than fit on screen, scroll Inbox, No date, a project, Completed, Browse, and search results to the very end. The last row sits fully above the tab bar and the `+` on iOS, and above the floating `Quick add` on a phone. `bun run test:e2e -- e2e/layout.e2e.ts` checks every web view, sheet, and dialog at 1280 px and 393 px.
- **Proof.** Save a screenshot of the Inbox with every section on each client.

## Gotchas

- The Inbox's day sections depend on the device clock and time zone. Seed dates relative to the run, not fixed calendar dates. To seed an overdue task through the UI, set the clock back a day, reload, add it, then restore the clock and reload.
- The app reads the clock every 15 seconds, so a clock change shows after a reload or within 15 seconds.
- A view that shows a spinner on local data fails the snappiness requirement.
